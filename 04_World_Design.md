# 04_World_Design.md - E'xploreMe World Design

## Immersive World Layout & Hierarchy

```
                                [ AI Research Facility ]
                                     (North: 0, 25)
                                           │
                                           │
[ Mobile Innovation Center ] ── [ Innovation District ] ── [ Hall of Milestones ]
       (West: -25, 0)                (Center: 0,0)              (East: 25, 0)
                                           │
                                           │
                                   [ Future Gateway ]
                                    (South: 0, -25)
                                           │
                                   [ Communication Nexus ]
                                    (Far South: 0, -40)
```

## Zones & Highlights

1. **Neon Gateway & Innovation District (0, 0)**:
   - Central neon monolith, spawn ring, and milestone timeline paths (Memory Lane).
   - Interactive signposts directing visitors to all core sectors.
2. **AI Research Facility (0, 25)**:
   - Holographic neural brain model, matrix code pillars.
   - Highlights: **AI Resume Builder** & AI research initiatives.
3. **Mobile Innovation Center (-25, 0)**:
   - Floating holographic smartphone pods.
   - Highlights: **Wake-up Darling**, **Paalkaran**, **VibeSync**, **SpendGuard**. Direct APK downloads & QR codes.
4. **Hall of Milestones (25, 0)**:
   - Cyber trophy sculptures, developer achievement badges, skill metrics.
5. **Future Gateway (0, -25)**:
   - Glowing stargate vortex portal previewing upcoming projects (**Inventory Management System**, **FillPill**, **Photographic Memory Game**).
6. **Communication Nexus (0, -40)**:
   - Holographic transmission tower with contact form connected to Firebase Firestore.
